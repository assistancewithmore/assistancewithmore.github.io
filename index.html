Assistance With More

Specialized consulting services in medical assistance, insurance support, invoice review, negotiation, and cost containment.

Working with an established network of companies, medical professionals and combining industry knowledge, AWM helps to connect global assistance companies with local services, logistics and finalise insurance claims with positive financial outcomes.

Our Services

Assistance With: Medical Information

Support with contact in the public and private sector for international travellers, admitted or seeking treatment covered privately or by their policy.

  • Hospital Contact
  • Medical report receipt and delivery
  • Local network and cashless billing access

Assistance With: Medical Appointments

Support with coordination of outpatient care for international travellers seeking treatment covered privately or by their policy.

  • Appointment organisation
  • Medical report receipt and delivery
  • Cashless billing access

Assistance With: Logistics

Support with transport including taxis and ground ambulance patient transport services.

  • Liaison with local supplier directly
  • Transport organisation with local supplier
  • Payment for, or organisation of payment terms directly with traveller

Assistance With: Cost Containment

Independent review and negotiation designed to identify reasonable and appropriate opportunities to manage medical costs.

  • Medical invoice and bill review
  • Identification of billing discrepancies or potential savings
  • Provider invoice negotiation
  • Cost analysis and benchmarking
  • Negotiation and resolution of outstanding medical charges

Assistance With: Network Advice

Practical, case-specific support for pre-deployment advice and guidance in specific locations including in-person audit and local resource information.

  • Third Party recommendations
  • Analysis and information on network access in third party country or location

Assistance With: Experience

Medical and insurance matters can become complicated quickly. Assistance With More provides knowledgeable, hands-on support to help clients understand the situation, identify options, and move toward resolution.

Like all medical assistance work, solutions are tailored to each case. Whether coordinating care, reviewing an invoice, communicating with a provider, or negotiating medical costs, the focus is practical solution and clear communication.
